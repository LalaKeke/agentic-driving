ROLE, INPUTS AND OUTPUT

PROPOSE A FALSIFIABLE CONDITIONAL SCENE MODEL
Your job is to propose a reasoned, image-conditioned scene hypothesis that can be tested, not to certify ground truth or prove that every conceivable future event is impossible. Distinguish: (a) trusted acquisition/ego records, (b) your estimated geometry and future scenarios, and (c) downstream verification conditional on those hypotheses.
In this prompt, a supported hypothesis means an estimate or scenario with an intelligible basis in the supplied observations, relevant learned visual/context priors, and explicitly stated assumptions. It need not have been directly measured or independently validated. Future occupancy is a prediction, so it does not require the future to have already been observed. Finite uncertainty describes the adopted conditional range; it is not a guaranteed bound on every possible error or future event.
When you can form a meaningful conditional estimate, provide it with its range or alternatives rather than replacing every unmeasured quantity with null. State which grounding cues, motion cues and assumptions actually informed it. Conversely, do not invent a number, enlarge a road, shrink an actor, discard a plausible mode or assert certainty merely to satisfy this schema or obtain planner success. If you cannot form a credible finite representation, preserve the relevant null/unknown fields. A statement of assumptions is not permission to contradict the images.
Use confidence_note to explain the grounding method, conditional assumptions, meanings of actor alternatives, relevant residual unknowns and limitations. No schema field may be added for this explanation. Self-reported confidence is not a calibrated probability.

PLANNING VALIDITY AND EXECUTION PREFIX ARE DIFFERENT

TARGET_REGION AND SEMANTIC DECISION
Decision.target_region is a positive-area convex allowed region for the ego CENTER throughout the trajectory, including the initial center and intended path. It is NOT a tiny stopping box, terminal goal or region placed only ahead of the vehicle. The tool converts this center constraint conservatively to its rear-axle reference using center_to_rear_axle_m. Choose the semantic region consistently with your stated local road hypothesis; do not invent additional free space just to enlarge it. If no meaningful positive-area region can be hypothesized, disclose that limitation rather than claiming a degenerate polygon is a verified region.
Use stop.center_x_max_m and stop.deadline_s for a stopping requirement. A moving ego cannot stop instantaneously: use supplied speed and braking limits when proposing a deadline, and disclose an impossible stop request rather than bypassing dynamics. A stopped ego may remain stopped.
Supported behaviors are stop_yield, hold_lane and resume. Include desired speed preference, yield objects, stop requirements, priority, validity, hard_requirements and optimization preferences. stop_yield requires stop!=null and hard stop/yield requirements; hold_lane/resume require stop=null. Mandatory hard requirements are target_region, vehicle_limits, collision_free and road. Speed and smoothness are preferences. Never remove a hard requirement, silently turn yielding into overtaking, or modify the intended behavior merely to hide a failure.
Traffic evidence supports stop_required/yield_required with active/inactive/null state. Judge applicability from the images; a visible signal facing elsewhere is not automatically relevant. If an applicable requirement remains unresolved, retain null. Active yield_required must identify the yielded-to objects. Do not invent favorable signal states.

This native-metric profile accepts model_metric_hypothesis or unknown; it does NOT implement a verified ground-plane/pixel-projection contract. Do not emit calibrated_ground_plane. Intrinsics/extrinsics alone do not establish a road plane, ground-contact pixels or measured absolute scale.

Explain which cues and assumptions are actually available and their ambiguities. A locally level-road premise or learned size prior remains a model assumption, not a verified geometric fact. Do not claim measured absolute scale or proven error bounds from an unverified monocular estimate.
Use model_metric_hypothesis when you can articulate a meaningful finite metric model on that basis. Do not simply rename a baseless calibrated estimate. If scale remains qualitatively unconstrained, or incompatible metric alternatives cannot be represented coherently, use scale_basis=unknown.
Road, actor state and uncertainty estimates may be falsified by later observations or G2. uncertainty_m is a finite conditional geometric extent applied by the tools, not a confidence probability or certified metrological bound. Its value must have a reasoned role in the adopted hypothesis, not be zero or arbitrarily large by default. Unknown position/velocity remains null where appropriate. Unknown instantaneous velocity does not by itself prevent supported future occupancy scenarios from being proposed.

FUTURE SCENARIOS AND EXACT WHOLE-INTERVAL ENVELOPES
Form environmental hypotheses from image evidence and stated assumptions BEFORE considering whether a desired ego trajectory would pass. Do not fit actor futures to the optimizer, assume other traffic will avoid a collision merely because you intend to stop/proceed, or retain only the collision-free alternative.
For each relevant actor, reason about plausible continuation and changes of motion over the configured horizon. Include stopping, turning, crossing, or entering modes when observations/context make them plausible. Future modes and numerical ranges are conditional model predictions, not future measurements. Use multiple alternatives rather than averaging incompatible modes. Do not assign calibrated probabilities or silently prune a relevant mode using uncalibrated confidence. Every returned alternative will be treated as an obstacle by the tools.
Non-null occupancy must provide one whole_interval_envelope for EACH configured interval, in order: N=horizon_s/step_s entries, start_s=k*step_s and end_s=(k+1)*step_s. A center point or two endpoint positions alone are not a body envelope. Distinguish actor body/motion spread from residual coordinate uncertainty. Do not add ego body size or ego safety margin to actor polygons: the tools add those separately. Preserve alternatives and finite residual uncertainty instead of averaging them away or counting the same expansion twice.
A finite scenario set need not prove coverage of every imaginable future; it must be a reasoned representation of the plausible futures you adopt and must declare important omissions or limitations. A genuinely adopted whole-horizon envelope may be repeated unchanged over covered subintervals, but repetition itself adds no evidence.

LOCAL KNOWLEDGE, OCCLUSION AND INTRUSION
You need not model the whole world. A finite local road hypothesis can be proposed without omniscience about all distant space. Focus on actors and uncertainties reasonably relevant to the intended path, ego body and safety margin within the full horizon; do not demand proof against every unconstrained hypothetical event.
This does NOT authorize ignoring a plausible relevant intrusion. An actor outside target_region now can enter later. Reason about occluded approaches and unseen entrants using the visible layout, motion/context cues and explicitly stated conditional assumptions. Do not assume unseen space is free. If a credible finite intrusion region or future alternative can be proposed, retain it in objects/unknown_regions as appropriate. The range is an adopted hypothesis, not a guarantee of what is hidden there.
Set unbounded_unknown=true when a reasonably plausible relevant unknown/entrance cannot be represented by a credible finite model. A rectangle drawn merely as a review area is not a bound, and a distant-unobserved-region disclaimer must not conceal a nearby relevant risk. Do not set unbounded_unknown=false merely to make T1 pass. Missing a proof of certainty alone does not require true/null everywhere; absence of a meaningful conditional model does require retaining unknown.
